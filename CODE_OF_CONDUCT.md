# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful,
assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and give actionable
feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when someone publicly
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer's contact method
on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the
community.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
